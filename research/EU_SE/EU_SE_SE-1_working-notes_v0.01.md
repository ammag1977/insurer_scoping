## Findings

### Motor TPL premium tax (skatt pa trafikforsakringspremie), S1
- **Scope:** premiums for motor TPL insurance (trafikforsakring) covering a risk located in Sweden, plus the motor insurance association's fee for uninsured vehicles (trafikforsakringsavgift) (s 1-2). Location of risk follows forsakringsrorelselagen 1 kap 17 s / the foreign insurers act 1 kap 7 s, i.e. Solvency II.
- **Taxpayer:** the insurer granting the motor TPL cover (s 3).
- **Tax representative:** a foreign insurer **without a Swedish branch** (FOS) must appoint a tax representative resident in Sweden or a Swedish legal entity (s 3). The representative reports and pays the tax and holds the records (SFL 6 kap 4 s). RepRequirement = **Mandatory** for FOS; Not applicable for HOME and branches.
- **Tax base:** **earned premium** (intjanad premie) for each calendar month, i.e. gross written premium before reinsurance plus or minus the change in unearned premium; liability arises at the end of each month (s 5-8).
- **Rates (s 10):** **32%** of the earned motor TPL premium; 22% of the uninsured-vehicle fee (the association only).
- **Class coverage:** class 10 (motor vehicle liability) only. No other classes are taxed (no general IPT).

### Filing and payment (SFL)
- **Return:** excise duties are reported in an excise return (punktskattedeklaration) for each reporting period (SFL 26 kap 6 s). The return states the gross tax, deductions and net tax (26 kap 22 s).
- **Period:** calendar month. Skatteverket may allow an annual period where net excise duty for the year is at most SEK 50,000 (26 kap 17 s).
- **Due date depends on the insurer's VAT position (P10 applies to weekend dates):**

  | Insurer's VAT position | SFL | Due date for month M |
  |---|---|---|
  | Not VAT-registered (common for insurers) | 26 kap 32 s | 12th of month M+1; July by 17 August; December by 17 January |
  | VAT-registered, VAT base over SEK 40m | 26 kap 30 s | 26th of M+1; November by 27 December |
  | VAT-registered, VAT base up to SEK 40m | 26 kap 26 s | 12th of M+2 (e.g. January by 12 March), with listed exceptions |

- **Payment:** to the tax account by the same date; the SFL payment chapters are still to read.
