## Findings

### Road traffic insurance tax (avgift pa trafikkforsikringer)
- **Legal basis now:** S1 s 1 (framework); the annual Storting resolution (EEA_NO-L002, 2026: FOR-2025-12-18-2756); the Excise Duties Regulation ch 3-23 (EEA_NO-L005).
- **Not held:** both the resolution and the regulation chapter. **Rates, period, due dates, liable party and exemptions under the current regime are a Gap** until they are supplied.
- **Future regime (S2 ch 25, not in force):**
  - tax on motor TPL for vehicles in the vehicle register, charged when the insurer's liability begins (s 25-1);
  - the Trafikkforsikringsforeningen and insurers required to be its members register in the excise register;
  - **insurers with no place of business or domicile in Norway register through a representative** (s 25-2), and insurer and representative are jointly liable (s 25-3);
  - exemptions for Svalbard, stolen vehicles and premium finally lost (ss 25-4 to 25-6).
- **Class coverage:** class 10 (motor vehicle liability) only.

### Natural perils scheme (naturskadeforsikring), S3 and S4
- **Pool membership:** compulsory for non-life insurers that cover natural perils with fire insurance in Norway, **regardless of where the head office is** (S3 s 4). HOME, FOE and FOS scopes.
- **Natural perils premium:** the fire sum insured × a premium rate set **each year by the board of Norsk naturskadepool**, the same for all policyholders. It is collected by the policyholder's own insurer and shown as a separate item on the policy (S4 s 2, s 22).
- **Not held:** the rate for 2025 and 2026 (Gap). The pool equalises claims between members (S3 s 4); members' administration costs are compensated at 0.005 per mille of fire sums insured at 1 July (S4 s 38).
- **Non-member insurers (UNAUTH scopes):** a policyholder who buys fire insurance from a non-member insurer pays a levy to the pool, based on the fire sum insured, and must notify the pool. Breach is fined (S3 s 4a).
- **Scope question:** the natural perils premium is a pool premium passed to the policyholder, not a tax. Is it in scope as a parafiscal levy?
- **Class coverage:** class 8 (fire and natural forces), the property fire covers only.
