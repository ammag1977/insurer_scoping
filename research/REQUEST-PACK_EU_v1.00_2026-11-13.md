# EU premium-tax research: request pack v1.00 (2026-11-13)

Validated tax information only, not tax advice.

This pack lists what is still needed to close the 19 country research runs (seed v1.67). Everything we could reach has already been fetched. What remains needs either a person to save a page that blocks automated access, or a written question to a levy body or tax authority.

How to use it:
- **Part A:** save each page by hand and upload it to the JDH Europe Evidence desk under the Download ID given, or attach it in chat.
- **Part B:** each block is a ready-to-send request. Send it in English, or ask me for a translation into the local language.
- The links in this pack come from the official sites themselves or from the research runs. No email address is given unless a run found it on an official page.

Where the reply goes in the seed: each item names the seed row (JurCode and tax name, or LegID) that the answer will update.
