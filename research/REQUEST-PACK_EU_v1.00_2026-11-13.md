# EU premium-tax research: request pack v1.00 (2026-11-13)

Validated tax information only, not tax advice.

This pack lists what is still needed to close the 19 country research runs (seed v1.67). Everything we could reach has already been fetched. What remains needs either a person to save a page that blocks automated access, or a written question to a levy body or tax authority.

How to use it:
- **Part A:** save each page by hand and upload it to the JDH Europe Evidence desk under the Download ID given, or attach it in chat.
- **Part B:** each block is a ready-to-send request. Send it in English, or ask me for a translation into the local language.
- The links in this pack come from the official sites themselves or from the research runs. No email address is given unless a run found it on an official page.

Where the reply goes in the seed: each item names the seed row (JurCode and tax name, or LegID) that the answer will update.

## Part B: requests to levy bodies and authorities

### B1. Czechia: Czech Insurers' Bureau (CKP)
Contact: https://www.ckp.cz/kontakt (members can also use the secure zone, https://www.ckp.cz/zabezpecena-zona)
Seed rows: EU_CZ "Czech Insurers' Bureau Damage-Prevention Fund Contribution" and "Membership Contributions"

> We act for an insurer that writes, or plans to write, Czech motor third-party liability insurance as a CKP member. Please send us:
> 1. The current Statute of the Bureau.
> 2. The members' assembly resolutions setting the membership contributions for 2025 and 2026 (Act 30/2024 s 54(2), s 57(4)(b), s 61(5)).
> 3. Your instructions for the quarterly instalments of the damage-prevention fund contribution (Act 30/2024 s 73(3)): the statement or form, if any; the bank account and payment reference; how the quarter's premium base is measured; and what a member should send for a quarter with no premium.
> 4. Whether the Bureau accepts a third-party service provider as the "person authorised to act with the Bureau" under s 52(2).

### B2. Hungary: MABISZ (Association of Hungarian Insurance Companies)
Contact: mabisz.hu
Seed rows: EU_HU "MABISZ Compensation Fund Contribution", "MABISZ Guarantee Fund Contribution", "MABISZ Operating Cost Contribution"

> Under Act LXII of 2009 (Kgfb) s 57, please confirm for 2025 and 2026:
> 1. The rules, amounts and payment dates for the Guarantee Fund contribution, the Compensation Fund contribution and the operating-cost contribution.
> 2. Whether the Compensation Fund contribution (0.25%) is currently suspended because the fund exceeds 6%.
> 3. The bank account and payment reference for each contribution.
> 4. The format for the 15 January premium report and the quarterly vehicle-count reports.

### B3. Hungary: National Tax and Customs Administration (NAV)
Contact: nav.gov.hu (general information line or the competent directorate)
Seed rows: EU_HU "Insurance Tax", "Supplementary Insurance Tax"

> 1. For insurance tax (Act CII of 2012), what should an insurer file for a month with no premium: nothing, form 2620 with zero values, or a "no liability" declaration under Art s 49(4)?
> 2. How does an insurer writing under freedom of services, with no Hungarian establishment and no Hungarian electronic ID, get electronic access to file form 2620, directly or through a registered representative?
> 3. Please confirm that the small-insurer bands in s 5(2) as amended from 2025-07-20 apply to returns due after that date.
