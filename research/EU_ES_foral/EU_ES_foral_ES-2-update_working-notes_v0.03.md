## 1. What changed

- Bizkaia model 480 (gap closed): OF 4.224/1998 (BOB 14, 22.1.1999) approves model 480. Art 2: insurers that made taxed operations in the year file it. Art 3: due in the first 25 calendar days of January, filed with the December 430 where one is due. The instructions list bases by class (taxed and exempt), boxes 70-81, and 81 = 75 + 78 + 80. OF 499/2006 (BOB 42, 1.3.2006) covers online filing of 430 and 480 (arts 1-3) and the 25-day January deadline (art 4). Under art 5, authorised filers (presentador autorizado), meaning professionals under collaboration agreements, can file for third parties.
- Bizkaia January due dates: DF 112/2009, additional provision one (consolidated to 1.1.2026), says that periodic returns and self-assessments falling due in January may be filed and paid until 31 January. This covers the 480 and the December 430. Recorded as Mirror. The prudent system date stays 25 January.
- Alava model 480 (gap closed): OF 1.486/1998 (BOTHA 147, 23.12.1998) approves model 480. "Segundo" names the filers and "Tercero" says the 480 is filed with the last 430 at collaborating banks, which electronic filing has replaced. OF 24/2023 (BOTHA 15, 6.2.2023) art 9 sets the due date as the whole of January (from January 2023 filings).
- Alava model 430 (new finding): OF 24/2023 art 10 rewrites OF 1.121/1997 art 4 as follows: due 25 days after the month; the July return in August or by 10 September; the December return during January. The page previously showed only 25 days for December.
- Alava channel (correction): the Hacienda Foral Calendario Fiscal 2026 (Cuestiones generales) says, under OF 603/2020, that electronic filing is compulsory for taxpayers with economic activities or corporate-tax filing in Alava, and for authorised professionals in the Registro de Autorizaciones (DF 50/2001). This covers 430 (self-assessments) and 480 (annual summaries). The receipt shows the date and time of filing. In-person filing is therefore no longer a route for insurers. OF 603/2020 itself is not held. Its reference (BOTHA 137, 2.12.2020) comes from the Basque Government monthly digest.
- Gipuzkoa NF 2/2005, current consolidation to NF 2/2025 (unofficial information text, Mirror):
  - art 47: a representative of non-residents is required only where the tax rules require it.
  - art 46.4: filing under social collaboration.
  - art 61.1: payment in the periods of each tax.
  - art 26.6: late interest = legal interest + 25%.
  - art 27.2: late-filing surcharges of 2% (within 3 months), 5% (months 4-12) and 10% (after 12 months), plus interest.
- Bizkaia NF 2/2005, Spanish consolidated text to 1.1.2026 (Mirror):
  - art 46: a representative domiciled in Bizkaia only where the tax rules require one.
  - art 45.4: filing under social collaboration.
  - art 60.1.a: payment in the periods of each tax.
  - art 26.3: late interest = legal interest + 25%, set at 4.0625% for 2026 by NF 7/2025.
  - art 27.2: surcharge of 5%, or 0.5% within 3 working days. Note: art 27 applies to declarations under the art 122 procedure. For a self-assessment not paid in time, the executive surcharge under art 28.2 (5%, or 0.5% if paid within 3 working days) appears to apply. Interest under art 26.7 is not charged if the return is filed and paid within six months without prior request. A second check is needed.
- Historical wording: both 480 orders still name fiscal representatives of EEA FOS insurers as substitute filers (VZ art 2 citing DFN 2/1997 art 9.2; AL "Segundo"). This predates Ley 20/2015 and rests on repealed articles. Recorded as historical; the representative requirement stays Silent.
- Gipuzkoa and Navarra 480 were already covered on the page (GU OF 994/1998 and OF 575/2022, by 31 January; NC OF 143/2022, during January, online). No new fetch was needed.
