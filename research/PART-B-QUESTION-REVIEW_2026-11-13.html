Part B question review

Every question we would put to a tax authority, fund or bureau, grouped by country and body, so you can trim before anything is sent. Scope: domestic insurers, branches (FOE) and freedom of services (FOS); unauthorised insurers are left out. Each body's minimum ask is its Key questions only.

Before you ask anyone

Ask the client first for fund and bureau rules

Motor guarantee funds and bureaus send contribution calls, circulars and account details to their members. A client that is already a member can usually forward them, which avoids writing to the body at all.

Nil returns can mostly be dropped

We prepare a nil return every period as a service principle. Only ask where you need the legal position or want to know whether the channel accepts one.

One question, many bodies: can a provider file?

The same question on whether a service provider may file for the insurer comes up in several countries. If you settle your operating model first, some of these become unnecessary.

Already answered or historical

Questions marked Drop are covered by texts we now hold, are historical, or concern something out of scope. They are listed for completeness only.
