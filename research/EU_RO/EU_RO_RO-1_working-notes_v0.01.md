### Policyholders' Guarantee Fund (FGA) contributions: Law 213/2015 arts 3, 6-9; Norma ASF 27/2024
- **Who pays (art 3):**
  - (a) Insurers authorised by the ASF, including the activity of their branches in other EU member states. HOME scope.
  - (b) EU insurers, for business through branches established in Romania, but only for the part of the branch's business not protected by the home state's guarantee scheme (art 3(2)). FOE scope, conditional.
  - EU insurers on freedom of services are **not liable**.
- **Basis:** gross premiums collected (prime brute incasate) from direct business, calculated separately for general and life insurance, from the monthly accounting records (art 6(1); Norma 27/2024 art 2(3)).
- **Rates for 2025 (Norma 27/2024 art 2):**
  - permanent contribution: **2% general insurance**, 0.4% life;
  - special contribution (art 6(5)): **5% general insurance**;
  - applies to premiums collected 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.
- **Rates for 2026:** the norm is not held (Gap). The desk note records a draft of 3% plus 2% general and 0.4% life.
- **Cap:** 10% of gross direct premiums collected (art 6(2)). The ASF may raise the rate during the year (art 6(4)).
- **Payment:** monthly, in lei, to the Fund's account, as set by ASF regulations (art 7(1)); contributions are not refundable (art 7(2)).
- **Reporting:** monthly reports to the Fund, with a declaration signed by the legal representative or the branch's authorised agent (art 8).
- **Arrears:** the Fund issues a claim title with 30 days to pay, after which it becomes enforceable (art 9).
- **Class coverage:** all non-life classes 1-18 (general insurance) at the general rates; the life rate is out of scope.
