## Findings

### Damage-prevention fund levy (fond zabrany skod), Act 30/2024 s 73
- **Payer:** every member of the Czech Insurers' Bureau (Ceska kancelar pojistitelu).
- **Rate:** **3% of annual motor TPL premium received** (prijate pojistne: a received or cash basis), for each calendar year (s 73(3)).
- **Payment:** quarterly instalments due **30 April** (Q1), **31 July** (Q2), **31 October** (Q3) and **31 January of the following year** (Q4) (s 73(3)). Under P10 a deadline on a weekend or public holiday is brought forward.
- **Use of the fund:** at least 60% goes to the fire and rescue services, at least 15% to the other rescue services and at least 15% to road-safety programmes (s 74(2)).
- **Applies to:** motor TPL business only. Class coverage: class 10 (motor vehicle liability) = 3%; every other class = Not applicable for this levy.

### Who is a member (taxpayer scope), s 52-53
- **Czech-authorised insurers:** membership starts when the CNB authorisation becomes enforceable (s 52(1)). HOME scope.
- **EU/EEA insurers writing motor TPL in CZ through a branch or on freedom of services:** membership starts on the day business actually starts (s 52(1)). FOE and FOS scopes are therefore liable for the 3% levy.
- **Notification:** EU/EEA insurers notify the Bureau in writing, without undue delay, of the expected start date, the actual start or end date (at the latest on that day), and the person authorised to act; and of changes to that person by the effective date (s 52(2)).
- **Who acts for the insurer:** until notified, the branch manager (FOE) or the claims representative for CZ (FOS) acts for the insurer with the Bureau (s 52(3)).
- **Claims representative:** FOS insurers must appoint one resident or established in CZ (s 35). This is a regulatory duty, recorded but not a tax filing.
- **Not liable:** UNAUTH scopes. Only Bureau members pay.

### Bureau membership contributions (clenske prispevky), s 54, s 61(5)
- **Basis:** a fixed amount per vehicle in the policy or a percentage of written premium (s 54(2)), set each year by the Bureau's members' assembly in the financial plan (s 61(5)), with extraordinary contributions possible (s 61(6)).
- **Insolvency share:** CNB-authorised members also fund insolvency compensation (s 54(1), (3)).
- **Scope decision for the PO:** this is a professional-body contribution set by the Bureau, not by statute. Is it in scope as a parafiscal levy (like Spain's Consorcio), or out of scope under rule 7?
