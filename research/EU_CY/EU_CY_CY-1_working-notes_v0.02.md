## Findings per tax/levy

### 1. Stamp duty on insurance policies (telos chartosimou), S1-S4 - applies to 2025 only
- **Charge (S1 art 4(1)):** every document listed in the First Schedule is dutiable if it relates to property
  situated in the Republic or to matters or things to be performed or done in the Republic, **irrespective of
  where it is executed**. A policy written by an FOS insurer abroad on a Cypriot risk is therefore within the
  charge. "Asfalistirion" covers any document concluding, agreeing or evidencing an insurance contract (art 2).
- **Amounts (First Schedule item 21, "Asfalistiria"), flat per policy:**
  - 21(2) fire etc., for every payment agreed as indemnity for loss of or damage to property in the Republic by
    fire **or otherwise**: **EUR 1** if the sum insured does not exceed EUR 1,700; **EUR 2** otherwise;
  - 21(3) marine insurance (art 2: ships, machinery, cargo, freight and other interests; also goods in transit,
    including non-marine risks of the transit): **EUR 1** if not exceeding EUR 350; **EUR 2** if over;
  - 21(4) workmen's compensation (employers' liability): **EUR 2**;
  - 21(5) compulsory motor third-party liability and air travel insurance (travel coupons): **EUR 2**;
  - 21(6) insurances of any other kind (excluding policies under the art 9 composition): **EUR 2**;
  - 21(1) life (out of scope): EUR 2 / 4 / 7 / 18 by sum assured.
  - Art 7: a document within two or more schedule items bears only the highest duty.
  - RateSourceDate: CyLaw consolidated text, last amended by 221(I)/2025 (E.E. 5066, 12.12.2025); item 21 not
    changed by 168(I)/2024 or 221(I)/2025 (S3 read). The date item 21's euro amounts were last set was not
    traced (Insurance Europe S13 says the schedule was revised 17.12.2012, in force 1.3.2013 = 173(I)/2012).
- **Accident policies, composition (art 9 and Second Schedule):** where the Commissioner (Eforos, the Director of
  the Tax Department) agrees with an issuer of accident policies, those policies bear no stamp; instead a tax of
  **5% of all premiums received** on accident policies is due as stamp duty (art 9(3)). Quarterly statements in
  the Commissioner's form, delivered **within 20 days from 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October**; tax paid on
  delivery (Second Schedule paras 1-4). Late statement or payment: **10%** of the tax plus **10% for each further
  month** (art 9(5)). Whether any insurer holds such an agreement is not known (Gap).
- **Taxpayer (art 29(c)):** absent agreement to the contrary, the duty on policies is borne by the **insured**.
  The insurer carries the compliance risk: anyone who receives a premium and does not issue a duly stamped policy
  within **two months**, or issues an unstamped policy, commits an offence, fine up to **EUR 100** (art 55). Every
  receipt of a renewal premium needs a duly stamped receipt within one month (art 30(2)).
- **How paid:** by stamps (adhesive or impressed) only, unless regulations allow cash payment (arts 11, 12, 64).
  Documents executed in Cyprus are stamped at or before signature (art 19); late stamping within 30 days at par,
  within 6 months with surcharges (EUR 2 surcharge where the duty is up to EUR 2), later at double (art 20).
  Documents executed abroad: within 30 days of receipt in Cyprus (art 21). Insurance Europe (S13, Secondary)
  says insurers pay policy duty **quarterly under a bilateral agreement with the Tax Department**; that agreement
  form is not held.
- **Repeal (S2):** N 239(I)/2025 s 2: "Oi peri Chartosimon Nomoi tou 1963 eos 2024 katargountai tin 1i Ianouariou
  2026", with 221(I)/2025 listed in the margin; the long title says "1963 eos 2025". Published E.E. Par. I(I)
  Ar. 5070, Wednesday 31.12.2025, p. 1247. No transitional provision in the act.
- **Transition (S4, Tax Department 7.1.2026):** documents drawn up and signed **by at least one party up to
  31.12.2025** remain dutiable and must be stamped under the existing procedures; documents signed after
  1.1.2026 are not dutiable. Policies (and renewals) signed in 2025 are in scope; 2026 policies are not.
  Revera (Secondary) reports a Tax Department announcement of 9.1.2026 moving stamping of pending documents to
  the TFA portal from 1.4.2026 (assessment online, payment, then stamping at District Offices) - not read on
  tax.gov.cy (blocked).
- **2025-2027 change:** abolished from 1.1.2026; no other change in 2025.

### 2. Motor Insurers' Fund premium surcharge (epasfalistro TAMO / MIF), S5-S8, S12
- **Body:** Tameion Asfaliston Michanokiniton Ochimaton, a company limited by guarantee registered 18.2.1969
  (no. 2377), also the Cyprus Green Card bureau (S5 art 2). It is not a public body; the ICCS does not collect.
- **Membership:** all insurers falling under the law (S5 art 27). FOS motor insurers must declare membership of
  the national guarantee fund and bureau before starting (S9 art 161(4)(b)); branches likewise (art 158(4)).
  FOS insurers also need a claims representative resident or established in Cyprus (art 165) - not a fiscal
  representative.
- **Legal basis of the charge:** the law sets no amount. Art 28(4) (added by 167(I)/2023 s 21, E.E. 4975,
  20.12.2023): the Fund's financing is set in the Basic Agreement so that the Fund has adequate resources. Basic
  Agreement para 3 (S6, _EN): the Fund is financed "by means of a policy surcharge or by any other means, to be
  collected from policyholders, through the Insurers", fixed in accordance with the **directions of the Minister
  of Finance** issued after consulting the Fund.
- **Payer:** economically the **policyholder** (separate surcharge on the motor policy, S6, S7, S12); the insurer
  collects and remits as unpaid collector (S12). For our model: taxpayer = insurer as collector (all active
  scopes writing class 10).
- **Rate - conflicting, no primary direction found:**
  - **5% of premium** from 1.1.2001 (3% before) - S8 (official RTD page, figures to 2005), S12 (2021 article,
    updated 8.7.2026: "not revised since"), S13 (Insurance Europe 2025);
  - **EUR 9 per vehicle and EUR 27 per motor trade policy from 2024, plus EUR 1 per vehicle / EUR 3 per motor
    trade policy for insurer insolvency** - S12 (17.4.2026, "until 2024 it was 5% of each motor policy's premium");
  - seed: EUR 10 per vehicle flat (consistent with 9 + 1).
  The Minister's direction (and any Gazette notice) setting the per-vehicle amounts was not found. Grade: Gap
  (Secondary only). RateSourceDate unknown.
- **Remittance:** "within two calendar months from the end of each quarter" (Internal Agreement para 10, quoted
  by S7 and S8). Internal Agreement (Esoteriki Symfonia) not held; no return form found.
- **UNAUTH scopes:** an insurer not authorised in Cyprus cannot lawfully write compulsory motor TPL there and is
  not a member; Not applicable (record, PO to confirm).
- **Not levies:** Cyprus Information Centre data supply (S5 art 22(3A), monthly within 15 days; EUR 200/day
  fine) is a reporting duty, not a charge; the Cyprus Hire and Rejected Risks Pool is a risk-sharing pool.

### 3. Other charges checked - none in scope
- No insurance premium tax on non-life. Life: minimum income tax of 1.5% of gross premiums (S13) - life, out of
  scope. No fire service contribution found (none in S9; S13 has no Cyprus entry).
- ICCS supervisory fees under regulations made under S9 art 38(18): regulatory, out of scope.
- Cap 199 "Insurance Premium Levy" (EU_CY-L002): Out of scope (PO B15); Cap 199 is repealed by 38(I)/2016.
- Eggyitiko Tameio Asfalisis Zois (S9 art 324A): life only, out of scope.
- S9 art 171(1) (transposing Solvency II art 157) subjects policies covering Cypriot risks to indirect taxes,
  stamp duties and parafiscal contributions "provided by the Income Tax Law 2002" and collected under that law.
  The cross-reference to the Income Tax Law looks like a drafting slip (the stamp duty sits in S1); record for
  the PO, no practical effect found.

## Location of risk
- **Transposition:** S9 art 2, definition "kratos melos opou vrisketai o kindynos" (a)-(d) = Solvency II art
  13(13): buildings and contents in the same policy - where situated; vehicles - state of registration; travel or
  holiday risks <= 4 months - where the policyholder signed; otherwise habitual residence, or for a legal person
  the establishment to which the contract relates.
- **Art 171(2) S9** = Solvency II art 157(2): movables in a building in Cyprus (except goods in commercial
  transit) are a Cypriot risk even if building and contents are not covered by the same policy.
- **National extension:** the stamp duty charge (S1 art 4(1)) is document-based ("property situated in the
  Republic or matters or things to be performed or done in the Republic"), not tied to the art 13(13) test. It
  may reach policies on risks located elsewhere under art 13(13) (e.g. a Cypriot-registered vehicle's policy
  is consistent; a policy issued in Cyprus covering property abroad is arguably outside "property situated in
  the Republic"). RuleCategory National extension, term ObjectInStateAtInception; ContraryToEU: possible for
  risks located in another EEA state under art 157 - flag for the PO (2025 only).
- MIF: follows the compulsory motor insurance of vehicles normally based in Cyprus (S5); no conflict found.

## Seed differences
1. **MIF local name wrong:** "Eisfora sto Tameio Apozimiosis Asfalismenon Aytokiniton (TAAA)" - no such body.
   Correct: "Epasfalistro Tameiou Asfaliston Michanokiniton Ochimaton (TAMO)" (Motor Insurers' Fund premium
   surcharge). "Aytokiniton" is also not ELOT 743.
2. **MIF Collected by:** seed "ICCS / Ministry of Finance" - wrong; collected by insurers and remitted to the MIF
   (private company limited by guarantee). The Minister sets the amount; ICCS does not collect.
3. **MIF amount:** seed "EUR 10 per vehicle flat fee ... at policy inception/renewal; no periodic return" -
   consistent with a Secondary report (EUR 9 + 1 insolvency, from 2024) but unsupported by any primary text;
   official RTD page and Insurance Europe say 5% of premium. Remittance is quarterly (2 months after quarter end),
   so "no periodic return" is doubtful.
4. **MIF Lines of business "4 Motor vehicle liability":** Solvency II class is **10**.
5. **EU_CY-L001 Role "Creates the tax":** N 96(I)/2000 does not create the charge; art 28(4) refers funding to
   the Basic Agreement. Propose Role "Legal basis (framework)" and add L007 (Creates the tax).
6. **Stamp duty local name "Telos Asfalistron"** - wrong; "Telos chartosimou (asfalistiria)". Seed "EUR 2 per
   policy" incomplete: EUR 1 or 2 by item, and 5% of premiums for composed accident policies (art 9).
7. **EU_CY-L004 citation:** "toy" -> "tou": "O peri Chartosimon Nomos tou 1963 (19/1963)". The desk copy (DL-037)
   is the 1963 original only; consolidated CyLaw copy now saved.
8. **EU_CY-L005 Greek title wrong:** correct "O peri Chartosimon (Katargitikos) Nomos tou 2025, N 239(I)/2025
