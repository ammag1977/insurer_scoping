## 2. Findings per tax / levy

### 2.1 Imposta sulle assicurazioni (insurance premium tax), State (Erario)
- **Regimes.** 2025-01-01 to 2026-12-31: L 1216/1961 arts 1-34 + Allegati A/B/C. From 2027-01-01: TU 174/2024 Titolo I (arts 1-23) + Allegato 1 Tab A/B/C (TU art 100 as amended by DL 200/2025 art 4(2)); TU art 99(1)(a) repeals L 1216 arts 1, 1-bis, 2-bis to 9, 11, 12, 16-23, 31-34 and Allegati. L 1216 arts 24, 26 repealed by D.Lgs 173/2024 art 101(1)(d) and arts 27-30 by D.Lgs 141/2026 art 367(1)(c), each from that TU's start date (2027) (S1 update list). Tariff article numbers are identical in both regimes (Tab A = "ex Tariffa Allegato A ... e successive modificazioni").
- **Taxpayer.** Insurer (L 1216 art 9; TU art 12). Right of recovery from policyholder unless agreed otherwise; tax shown separately (art 17; TU art 16). Policyholder pays where insurer is "all'estero" (art 11; TU art 13) - see 3.
- **Base.** Premium collected ("incassati"), in Italy or abroad, with all accessories, no deductions; excludes only the tax recovered from the policyholder (art 4; TU art 5). Tax-exclusive. Due as premium is paid; not refunded if premium returned. Reinsurance taxed only where original cover untaxed (art 3; TU art 4). EU co-insurance: leader established in Italy pays on whole premium; non-established leader appoints representative (art 6-bis; TU art 9).
- **Rates (L 1216 Allegato A as amended = TU Tab A; AdE model lines AB1-AB30).** 2.50%: art 5 accident/sickness, 8A/8B crops and livestock, 10A-C nuclear, 14 employment risk. 4.38%: cumulative accident (AdE Circ 47/1983, model AB7). 7.50%: art 2A/2B marine, inland, aviation (0.05% for maritime risks of ships registered in Italy, DL 457/1997 art 9-quater; TU art 1(5)). 10.00%: art 24 assistance (CAP art 353(2)). 12.50%: art 3A/3B land transport and baggage, 8C/8D farm liability and farm fire, 12 suretyship, 13 credit, 16 licence withdrawal, 19 motor (L 1216 art 1-bis; TU art 2(1)). 21.25%: art 4 general liability, 6 theft, 7A/7B fire, 9 machinery/erection, 11 film, 15 legal expenses, 17 glass, 18 rain, 20/21 combined buildings/household, 22 residual. Tab B: 0.62% (arts 1-2) and 4.25% (art 3).
- **Motor change 2026 (S7).** L 199/2025 c 60(a) adds to L 1216 art 1-bis(1) (c 61(a) to TU art 2(1)): driver accident and roadside assistance are taxed at 12.5% "a prescindere dalla distinta indicazione nel contratto"; c 60(b)/61(b) replace tariff art 19: 12.50% on all risks inherent to the vehicle or craft "compresi quelli di cui agli artt. 4, 5, 6, 7, 15, 17 e 24", even if not in the same contract as compulsory TPL. Applies to contracts made or renewed from 2026-01-01 (c 64). c 59 (authentic interpretation, earlier contracts): driver accident and roadside assistance with a separately shown premium are NOT "other vehicle risks" (so 2.5% / 10%). c 62: tax on such premiums collected Jan-May 2026 payable by 30-06-2026. c 63: insurers must pass back at least two-thirds of the extra tax to the policyholder. **IT-1 correction:** IT-1 stated the c 59 rule as the 2026 rule; it is the opposite.
- **Exemptions.** Tab C (life, INAIL, INPS, mutual aid, export credit, etc.); residential catastrophe cover for policies from 2018-01-01 (Tab C art 11-bis, renumbered 12 by D.Lgs 141/2026 art 5(1)(v)); cultural-heritage property; farmers' collective defence bodies (TU art 1(6)-(7)).
- **Rate changes 2025-2027:** only the 2026 motor scope change. No rate changes in TU Tab A.

### 2.2 Imposta RC auto - Province (provincial motor TPL tax)
- **Nature.** Insurance tax on compulsory motor TPL premiums (mopeds excluded) is a "tributo proprio derivato" of provinces and metropolitan cities from 2012 (D.Lgs 68/2011 art 17(1); TU art 2(4) from 2027). Revenue goes to the province where the vehicle is entered in the PRA (public motor register); for farm machinery, the province of residence of the registration-certificate holder (D.Lgs 446/1997 art 60(1)). F24 practice: field "prov" = province of residence of the registration-certificate holder (Ris 109/E/2010) - normally the same as the PRA province; record both, PO note.
- **Rate.** 12.5% base; each province may raise or lower by up to 3.5 points (D.Lgs 68/2011 art 17(2); TU art 2(5)). A change takes effect on the 1st day of the second month after publication on finanze.gov.it (publication is a condition of effect). Sicily: TU art 2(8) / D.Lgs 68/2011 art 17 cross-refer; yet MEF lists rates for all 9 Sicilian provinces (all 16%) - flag.
- **How to find the rate.** MEF live CSV (S25), saved 13-11-2026: 100 entries. 96 at 16.0%; Aosta 9.0%; L'Aquila 15.5%; Treviso 15.0%; Sondrio 12.5%. Differentiated 9% for rental vehicles without driver in Roma and Firenze. Recent changes in force from 2027-01-01 (published Nov 2026): Agrigento, Lodi, Pavia, Pisa, Pistoia, Novara, Torino (all 16%). **Not in the list:** Trento, Bolzano (D.Lgs 446/1997 art 60(4): autonomous provinces apply their own statutes) and the Friuli Venezia Giulia areas (Gorizia, Pordenone, Trieste, Udine). Their rate source = Gap.
- **Motor TPL not provincial:** mopeds (ciclomotori) and craft (natanti) compulsory TPL remain State tax at 12.5% (model line AB15 "Erario"); motor other risks (art 19) are State tax at 12.5%.
- **Filing.** Same denuncia, Quadro AP per province (premiums, rate, tax, payments); plus the per-contract file of amounts paid to provinces (D.Lgs 68/2011 art 17(4)), also for FOS insurers (S19, S21).

### 2.3 Contributo al Servizio Sanitario Nazionale (SSN) on motor and craft TPL
- 10.5% of motor-vehicle and craft TPL premiums collected; shown separately on policy and receipts; recoverable from policyholder (CAP art 334(2)). Reported and collected under L 1216 (art 334(3)) - in the same annual return (Quadro AS) and paid by F24 to AdE, NOT to CONSAP.
- Advance 85% of previous year's contribution by 16 November; may be offset from the following February (CAP art 334(3)).
- Destination split: Erario (code 3357), Friuli Venezia Giulia (3358, L 448/2001 art 52(4)-(5)), Valle d'Aosta (3363, L 690/1981 art 10), Trento (3359) and Bolzano (3360) for vehicle-registration holders resident there (L 191/2009 art 2(110)). Model Quadro AS sections I-V.
- Non-deductible for income tax and IRAP (DL 102/2013 art 12(2-bis), CAP art 334 note 29).

### 2.4 Contributo al Fondo di garanzia per le vittime della strada (CONSAP road victims fund)
- Payer: insurers authorised for motor/craft compulsory TPL, paid annually to CONSAP (CAP art 285(3)); cap 4% of taxable premium (art 285(4)). FOS motor insurers must adhere to the Fund (CAP art 24(1)).
- Rate: 2.50% of premiums collected in the year, net of the IVASS management-cost deduction - 2025: DM MIMIT 30-12-2024 (IVASS provv 153/2024); 2026: DM MIMIT 22-12-2025, GU SG 302 of 31-12-2025 (IVASS provv 166/2025). 2027 decree not yet published (due by 31 December).
- Schedule (DM art 2, under DM 28 aprile 2008, n 98, art 8(2)-(3)): provisional contribution by 31 January of the year on premiums in the last approved accounts; adjustment by 30 September after approval of that year's accounts.
- Not on the AdE return; payment channel to CONSAP and the IVASS deduction % = Gap.

### 2.5 Contributo al Fondo di garanzia per le vittime della caccia (hunting victims fund)
- Payer: insurers authorised for compulsory hunting liability; annual payment to CONSAP; cap 15% (CAP art 303(3)-(4)).
- Rate: 10% of premiums collected net of IVASS management-cost deduction for 2025 (DM 30-12-2024, GU SG 305 of 31-12-2024) and 2026 (DM 22-12-2025, GU SG 302 of 31-12-2025). Schedule: provisional by 31 January, adjustment by 30 September (DM 98/2008 art 31(2)-(3)).

### 2.6 Contributo al Fondo di solidarieta per le vittime delle richieste estorsive e dell'usura (anti-racket)
- **Live rate provision (B45).** L 44/1999 art 18(1)(a): contribution "determinato ai sensi del comma 2" on premiums collected in Italy in the rami fire, other liability (RC diversi), motor other risks (auto rischi diversi) and theft, for contracts from 1-1-1990. Art 18(2) keeps "la misura percentuale prevista dall'articolo 6, comma 2, del DL 419/1991", re-determinable by Interior Ministry decree. DL 419/1991 art 6(2) (text in force before the repeal of Capo I): the tax on those rami "e aumentata dell'uno per cento". So the measure survives by express reference in L 44/1999 art 18(2) although art 6 itself is repealed.
- **Rate 1% of premiums** (AdE model line AC12, printed rate "1"; F24 code 3361 "aumento aliquota", S22). It is an increase of the insurance tax rate on those lines (21.25% + 1 point; ARD 12.5% + 1 point). No Interior Ministry re-determination decree found (search not exhaustive: Gap-check).
- Filed in the same denuncia (Quadro AC Sezione II) and paid monthly with the tax (code 3361). Seed rows grade "Collected by CONSAP" is wrong.

### 2.7 Contributo antincendi Trentino-Alto Adige (Cassa regionale antincendi)
- Fire insurers operating in the region pay, on contracts for property in the region, a contribution not recoverable from the insured, "pari alla misura stabilita dalle leggi dello Stato" on premiums collected (L.R. 24/1954 art 32). Premiums declared by 31 January; amount, payment method and terms fixed each year by decree of the President of the Regional Giunta.
- Deductible from the insurance tax on fire and combined policies (L 1216 art 32(3); TU art 23; model line AC2). Economic effect: part of the 21.25%, not an extra cost.
- Bolzano: L.R. 24/1954 is replaced in the province of Bolzano by L.P. 18 dicembre 2002, n 15 (art 56) (S16 footnote 3); whether a Bolzano contribution exists = Gap. Rate law (L 13 maggio 1961, n 469, art 4) and annual decree = Gap.

- **EU-law position:** a mandatory resident tax representative for EU FOS insurers was held contrary to art 56 TFEU in C-678/11 Commission v Spain (11-12-2014) (Secondary: official text not reachable). Italy already exempted EU/EEA insurers in 2009 (art 4-bis(6-bis)); no conflict remains for EU/EEA. EEA states qualify only if they "assicurano un adeguato scambio di informazioni" (not further defined): PO note.
- **Provider filing:** the denuncia is sent electronically by the declarant or by authorised intermediaries under DPR 322/1998 art 3(2-bis),(3) (professionals, trade associations, CAF, others listed) (S19, Primary; DPR text not read). The transmitting intermediary need not be the fiscal rep (Ris 80/E/2010). A fiscal rep may be a company (instructions: "rappresentante sia una societa"); no licensing requirement for the rep is stated in L 1216 beyond residence. A captive manager can be fiscal rep if resident in Italy, but can transmit only if it is an authorised intermediary; otherwise transmission via an Italian tax professional.
- **Registration:** written notice to AdE within 30 days of starting business (operations, seats, representatives; changes within 30 days) (art 20; TU art 19); penalty EUR 250-1,000 (art 24(1)(n)). Italian codice fiscale needed for return and F24 (Ris 80/E/2010 allowed the IVASS register number transitionally).

## 6. Location of risk
- L 1216 art 1 / TU art 1(1): (a) immovable property and contents not in commercial transit - where situated; (b) vehicles, ships, aircraft registered in Italy; (c) travel/holiday cover of 4 months or less - where signed; (d) goods transported to or from Italy, for persons domiciled in Italy, unless tax paid abroad; (e) other non-life - policyholder's domicile, or for legal persons the seat or establishment to which the contract relates "o cui sono addette le persone assicurate".
- CAP art 1(1)(fff) transposes Solvency II art 13(13) faithfully, and adds 4-bis (dispatched vehicle: state of registration or, for 30 days from acceptance, destination) and 4-ter (vehicle without plates: state of accident).
- Differences to flag (record both, PO): (i) art 1(d) is a national rule for goods in transit (tax only if not taxed abroad) - narrower or different from 13(13)(d); (ii) art 1(e) "establishment to which the insured persons are attached" extends the residual rule (group accident/sickness); (iii) L 1216 art 1(b) does not cover CAP 4-bis dispatched vehicles (not registered in Italy): possible gap vs EU rule; (iv) art 1(c) "stipulate nel territorio della Repubblica" matches 13(13)(c).
